## NeoForge note

Due to the effort of building and maintaining NeoForge versions, they will no longer be released for non-LTS versions.  
In the case of Advantimations, this means that NeoForge versions will only be developed for 26.1, although the newer LTS versions will also be supported.  
The architecture to support NeoForge will not be removed, but it will only be maintained per LTS version.
